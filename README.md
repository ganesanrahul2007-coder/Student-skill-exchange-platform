Problem Statement



Students have different skills, but they may not know who can teach

them the skill they want to learn.



For example, one student may know Python and another student may know

UI Design.



Our platform helps these students find each other and exchange their

skills.



In simple words, students can teach what they know and learn what they

want.





Objective



The main objective of this project is to connect students who can

teach and learn different skills.



The platform helps students to



Find students with useful skills



Share the skills they know



Find the skills they want to learn



Find suitable skill exchange partners



Send and accept skill exchange requests



Give ratings and feedback

RAG Chatbot



Our platform includes an AI chatbot called SkillMate AI.



The chatbot helps students understand the platform and provides

answers using relevant information from the knowledge base.



RAG means Retrieval Augmented Generation.



The basic process is



User asks a question



Question is processed



Relevant information is retrieved



The information is given to the AI model



AI generates the answer
